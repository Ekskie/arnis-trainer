# 🎥 Arnis Master Video Demonstrations

Place your demonstration video recordings for the 12 strikes of Arnis in this directory.

### Recommended File Naming
- `strike_1.mp4` - Strike 1: Left Temple
- `strike_2.mp4` - Strike 2: Right Temple
- `strike_3.mp4` - Strike 3: Left Torso
- `strike_4.mp4` - Strike 4: Right Torso
- `strike_5.mp4` - Strike 5: Abdomen Thrust
- `strike_6.mp4` - Strike 6: Left Chest Thrust
- `strike_7.mp4` - Strike 7: Right Chest Thrust
- `strike_8.mp4` - Strike 8: Left Knee
- `strike_9.mp4` - Strike 9: Right Knee
- `strike_10.mp4` - Strike 10: Left Eye Thrust
- `strike_11.mp4` - Strike 11: Right Eye Thrust
- `strike_12.mp4` - Strike 12: Crown Strike

### Video Format Recommendations
- **Container:** `.mp4` (H.264 video codec, AAC audio or muted)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 or 9:16 (vertical mobile orientation works best)
- **Duration:** 3 to 6 seconds per strike showing Chamber (Kasa), Strike Apex (Tudla), and Recovery (Bawi)

You can also specify remote HTTPS video URLs in `constants/strikeVideos.ts`.
